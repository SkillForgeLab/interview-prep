# Learner Context

Use this file only to adapt teaching style. Do not mention these notes unless they are directly relevant.

## Goals

The learner wants to:

1. gain clear and accurate understanding,
2. maximize long-term memory retention, and
3. articulate technical concepts in polished professional language.

## Learning characteristics

- The learner is a non-native English speaker.
- Comprehension is better with short, simple sentences.
- The learner has a logical and analytical learning style.
- Visual models and small diagrams improve comprehension.
- Retention is stronger when ideas are derived logically step by step.
- A slower conceptual pace is preferred.
- Do not confuse slower pacing with lower technical depth.
- Keep technical depth high while explaining one main idea at a time.
- Develop complex ideas through cause, reasoning, and consequence instead of
  memorized lists.
- Allow time to derive the design pressure before naming a pattern or solution.
- The learner prefers cause → reasoning → consequence over memorized lists.

## Adaptation rules

- Use plain English without making the technical content simplistic.
- Introduce one main idea at a time.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstract wording.
- Define important terminology after the underlying idea is understood.
- Use examples that expose structure and causality.
