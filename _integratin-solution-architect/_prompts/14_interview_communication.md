# Handoff brief 14 — Interview communication and career stories

Create a **standalone study guide** titled *Communicating Enterprise Integration Architecture in Interviews*. The
audience is a senior/principal architect with around 20 years of experience. Aim for roughly 2,000–3,000 words. This is
a practice guide, not a technical encyclopedia. Do not invent the reader's experience or metrics; provide fill-in
frameworks for real examples.

**Focus:** A 60-minute whiteboard flow: 0–10 requirements/NFRs, 10–25 high-level design, 25–40 choices and alternatives,
40–50 failure/security/scale tests, 50–60 delivery and operations. Adapt the same design for executive sponsor,
architecture review board, and engineering team. Make trade-offs, rejected alternatives, assumptions, and measurable
outcomes visible.

**Include:**

1. A minute-by-minute facilitation script and a reusable nine-part response outline from business goal through risks and
   next decisions.
2. A sample answer to an ambiguous enterprise integration prompt, showing what to say while drawing and when to pause
   for clarification.
3. Three versions of a short explanation of the same architecture: executive, review board, engineering team.
4. Three career-story worksheets: difficult architecture choice; production failure/mistake; business-technical
   conflict. Each must elicit situation, personal role, options, decision, outcome, evidence, and learning without
   fabricating details.
5. Mock-interview follow-ups, a self-review rubric, and a checklist for avoiding product buzzwords, excessive detail,
   and unsupported claims.

Include practice prompts that force the reader to defend cost, failure handling, migration, and stakeholder decisions.
