# Handoff brief 13 — Architecture leadership and governance

Create a **standalone study guide** titled *Architecture Leadership for Enterprise Integration*. The audience is a
senior/principal architect with roughly 20 years of experience. Aim for roughly 2,500–3,500 words, focused on judgment,
delivery, and organizational constraints. Use realistic scenarios and label assumptions; avoid invented career
achievements, prices, or employer policies.

**Focus:** Architecture principles; option analysis; ADRs; standards and exception processes; architecture review
boards; data and API ownership; stakeholder alignment; risk registers; platform selection and rationalization; build
versus buy; licensing and total cost; sequencing and delivery governance; cross-team conflict; incident accountability
and learning.

**Include:**

1. A worked decision where a proposed platform costs twice the budget. Compare scope, reuse, operating model, and risk
   options, then show how to communicate a decision.
2. A governance plan for ten teams with no shared data ownership: decision rights, contract ownership, standards,
   exceptions, and a gradual adoption path.
3. A short sample ADR with context, options, decision, rejected alternatives, consequences, and review trigger.
4. A production financial discrepancy scenario: investigation, business control, remediation, root-cause review, and
   prevention.
5. Questions and model answers for engineering disagreement, executive pressure, and standards exceptions, plus a
   self-scoring rubric.

Do not substitute committee process for technical reasoning. Explain how governance helps teams deliver safely and how
exceptions are handled without hiding risk.
