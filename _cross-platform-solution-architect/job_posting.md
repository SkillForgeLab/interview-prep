## JOB DESCRIPTION & RESPONSIBILITIES

- "Sr Solutions Architect-I" leads alignment of technology platforms with company roadmaps, optimizes integration and
  performance, oversees solution architecture and application programming interface strategy, mentors engineering teams,
  and collaborates on technical strategy and improvements.

- Mentors junior Solutions Architects and cross-functional team of colleagues, fostering a culture of excellence and
  innovation Identifies and implements technical solutions and business process improvements by analyzing business needs
  and leveraging innovative technologies.

- Collaborates with business partners and product engineering teams to enforce architecture practices, ensuring
  alignment with architectural and technical strategy through governance and project reviews.

- Leads technology platform solutions alignment with platform blueprints and company roadmaps, serving as the Single
  Point of Contact (SPOC) and coordinating with relevant stakeholders.

- Optimizes cross-platform integration implementing advanced integration strategies to enhance platform capabilities,
  performance, and efficiency.

- Ensures the engineered environment meets comprehensive business, application design, and infrastructure requirements
  by overseeing platform integration performance.

- Executes solution architecture design strategies, leads design and code reviews to ensure the delivery of desired
  business outcomes and adherence to enterprise standards.

- Supervises the platform's application programming interface strategy and implementation roadmap by collaborating with
  business and data architects, incorporating their input into cohesive platform solutions.

- Collaborates and co-creates effectively with teams in product and the business to align technology initiatives with
  business objectives.

- Embeds AI-assisted architecture tools into team workflows, improving design analysis, documentation, and platform
  integration outcomes.

- Mentors junior architects on responsible use of AI-enabled tools for solution design, API strategy, and requirement
  analysis.

- Applies AI-assisted capabilities to improve architecture reviews, business process analysis, and technical solution
  recommendations across the platform.

## QUALIFICATIONS

- Hands-on programming experience relevant to the assigned technology platform in a professional environment, and/or
  comparable experience

- Experience in application solution architecture design and end-to-end software development of complex distributed (
  multi-tiered) systems

- Experience in multiple technology disciplines with a deep knowledge of architectural concepts (business, data,
  technical, and solution) and a proven track record of implementation

- Experience in using a systems analysis and design methodology applicable to an Agile product environment

- Experience in relevant software development life cycle methodologies, practices, and compliance policies/procedures

- Experience in solution architecture, complex application systems design, and platform integration via modern
  approaches (i.e., RESTful application programming interfaces)

- Experience leading adoption of AI-assisted tools within solution architecture or platform integration teams

- Experience using AI-enabled capabilities to improve architecture analysis, design quality, and documentation practices

- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, and/or comparable experience;
  advanced degree preferred

- Have passed (or pass within 6 months) a Senior Engineer level DT Technical Assessment in at least one technology
  discipline relevant to the assigned Technology Platform(s)

- Knowledge of relevant supporting architecture domains required to align and deliver solutions (business, information,
  application, technical, etc.)

- Knowledge of Service Oriented Architecture design principles, execution patterns, and performance optimization
  techniques

- Knowledge of current industry best practices regarding solution delivery disciplines

- Knowledge of AI-enabled architecture tools and their application within solution delivery, integration, and
  documentation workflows

- Understanding of governance considerations for AI-assisted architecture work, including validation of outputs and
  control of design risk
