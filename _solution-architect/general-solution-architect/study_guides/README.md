# Solution Architect Interview Study Guides

This series turns the general Solution Architect preparation prompts into focused technical learning guides. Each
guide can be read independently, but the order below builds from requirements and system boundaries toward platform
choices, leadership, and interview practice.

The examples are illustrative. Use verified personal experience for behavioral answers, and label assumptions in
hypothetical designs. The series is provider-neutral unless a scenario explicitly names a platform.

## Guides

1. [Distributed Application Design](01_Distributed_Application_Design_for_Solution_Architect_Interviews.md) — responsibilities, boundaries, communication, and failure.
2. [Requirements, Quality Attributes, and Capacity](02_Requirements_Quality_Attributes_and_Capacity.md) — measurable goals, constraints, and first-order estimates.
3. [Data Architecture and Database Selection](03_Data_Architecture_and_Database_Selection.md) — ownership, invariants, workloads, and lifecycle.
4. [Messaging and Event-Driven Architecture](04_Messaging_and_Event_Driven_Architecture.md) — interaction models, delivery, replay, and backpressure.
5. [Application, API, and Integration Architecture](05_Application_API_and_Integration_Architecture.md) — boundaries, contracts, compatibility, and partners.
6. [Scalability and Performance](06_Scalability_and_Performance.md) — measurement, bottlenecks, and scaling tradeoffs.
7. [Reliability, Observability, and Disaster Recovery](07_Reliability_Observability_and_Disaster_Recovery.md) — objectives, failure behavior, and tested recovery.
8. [Security Architecture, Privacy, and Compliance](08_Security_Privacy_and_Compliance.md) — threat-driven controls, ownership, and evidence.
9. [Cloud, Infrastructure, and Cost](09_Cloud_Infrastructure_and_Cost.md) — operating models, foundational controls, and cost.
10. [Enterprise Modernization and Architecture Roadmaps](10_Enterprise_Modernization_and_Roadmaps.md) — migration, coexistence, and phased change.
11. [Architecture Governance, Leadership, and Agile Delivery](11_Architecture_Governance_Leadership_and_Agile_Delivery.md) — alignment, decisions, and delivery.
12. [Responsible AI-Assisted Architecture and Senior Technical Assessment](12_Responsible_AI_and_Senior_Technical_Assessment.md) — validation, privacy, and code-to-architecture reasoning.
13. [System Design Practice and Interview Synthesis](13_System_Design_Practice_and_Interview_Synthesis.md) — scenario practice, experience stories, and mock interviews.

## Suggested study flow

Start with guides 1–5 to establish the system model, constraints, data, communication, and contracts. Use guides 6–9
to deepen quality-attribute decisions. Then study modernization and leadership (10–11), responsible AI and technical
assessment (12), and apply the material through the capstone (13). Revisit the topic guides when a practice answer
reveals a gap.
