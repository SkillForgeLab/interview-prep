# Handoff brief 10 — Legacy modernization

Create a **standalone study guide** titled *Modernizing Legacy Enterprise Integrations Safely*. The audience is a senior/principal Enterprise or Integration Solution Architect in vendor-neutral interviews. Aim for roughly 3,000–4,000 words because this is a P0 area. Use concrete examples, label assumptions, and cite current primary sources for changing product claims.

**Focus:** Inventorying applications, interfaces and dependencies; prioritizing by criticality and fragility; Strangler Fig; anti-corruption layers; contract and adapter standards; coexistence; incremental migration; parallel and dual runs; testing; reconciliation; controlled cutover; rollback; organizational ownership; and risk management.

**Include:**

1. A phased plan for replacing 50 point-to-point integrations. Show discovery, target principles, migration waves, dependencies, governance, and measurable exit criteria.
2. A worked example replacing a critical legacy interface while retaining compatibility for consumers that cannot move yet.
3. A migration-wave scorecard using business value, risk, coupling, change window, and operational readiness.
4. A dual-run reconciliation and cutover checklist that includes rollback triggers and data correction ownership.
5. Interview follow-ups about a CTO's one-year deadline, an unchangeable critical interface, budget pressure, and production incidents, with model answers and an exercise.

Do not propose an all-at-once rewrite without showing why it is justified and how its risk is controlled. Discuss when leaving an interface in place temporarily is the responsible decision.
