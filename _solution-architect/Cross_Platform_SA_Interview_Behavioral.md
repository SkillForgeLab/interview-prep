# American Express solution architect interview prep: cross-platform projects

## 1. Behavioral stories to prepare from your actual experience

Prepare **six real stories**, each in two-minute and five-minute versions. Use Situation → Task → Actions → Result →
Reflection. Do not invent metrics: use a verifiable range or a qualitative outcome when exact numbers are unavailable.

1. **Cross-team integration:** Multiple platform owners, conflicting dependencies, how you secured a contract and
   delivery sequence.
2. **Architecture tradeoff:** Two viable designs, decision criteria, evidence, outcome, what you would change.
3. **Production incident:** Your role in stabilization, diagnosis, communication, and durable prevention.
4. **Security or risk decision:** A control or privacy issue you found early and resolved without losing the business
   goal.
5. **Influence and mentoring:** How you changed an engineering decision or raised a team’s design quality without formal
   authority.
6. **Delivery under ambiguity:** How you turned vague requirements into decisions, milestones, and measurable acceptance
   criteria.

**Result sentence template:** “We changed ___, which moved ___ from ___ to ___ over ___; the evidence was ___. The
remaining limitation was ___.”

Amex has publicly described leadership as enterprise thinking, collaboration, candid communication, effective decisions,
coaching, and living its values. Reflect those behaviors through concrete stories rather than reciting a values
list. [Source: American Express leadership framework](https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/newsroom/pdfs/AmericanExpress_DEI-Report_2022.pdf)
