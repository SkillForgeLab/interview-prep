# Professional Introduction: Solutions Architect, Cross-Platform Projects

I'm a senior engineer with extensive experience in commercial payments, reconciliation, and client-servicing platforms. My background combines solution design, hands-on engineering, and end-to-end delivery across interconnected enterprise systems.

At American Express, I modernized payment platforms and built integration solutions connecting enterprise systems. I aligned teams on solution design, technical constraints, and integration requirements, while coordinating delivery across platforms.

I worked closely with product owners, UX designers, architects, and engineers to translate business requirements into practical technical solutions. That meant balancing business priorities with technical feasibility and identifying opportunities to reuse capabilities or build on work already underway. I've also led globally distributed teams through design, development, testing, rollout, and production support.

My technical expertise includes Java and Vert.x-based services, API development and integration, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and operational automation. To support operational needs, I've used distributed tracing to follow requests across services and diagnose production issues. This hands-on foundation helps me build security, reliability, and observability into the design.

For this solutions architect role, I bring a combination of business understanding, cross-platform solution design, and delivery leadership. My strength is connecting the broader architecture with the implementation detail needed to build, release, and support the solution in production.

---

One example was partnering with the portfolio architect on a cross-platform architecture proposal for commercial payments. That work resulted in a $2.35 million product-funded release strategy. I also led roadmap execution for migrating BTA-Connect clients from a third-party application to an in-house reconciliation platform, reducing vendor dependency and improving operational control.

Earlier, at Verian, I led architecture and delivery of e-invoicing solutions and helped transform an installer-based Purchase-to-Pay product into a cloud-hosted service.
